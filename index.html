Customer analytics

Customer Churn Analysis

Investigated churn drivers across 7,043 telecom customers using Google Sheets for EDA and Power BI for the dashboard. Built DAX measures to calculate churn rate dynamically and created a fully interactive report with contract, tenure, charges, and service breakdowns.

↘ Month-to-month customers churn at 43% — 15× higher than two-year contract customers at 3%
↘ 47% of customers leave within their first year — the highest-risk window for any retention strategy
◆ Fiber optic customers churn at 43.4% despite paying premium prices — a clear service satisfaction gap
